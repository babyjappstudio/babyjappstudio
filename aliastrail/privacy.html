AliasTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

AliasTrail is a product of BabyJ App Studio LLC. This policy explains how AliasTrail and its product pages handle information.

The company-wide policy for other BabyJ App Studio products is available on the Privacy Policy page.

Local-first design

AliasTrail is designed so the aliases, sender notes, and related records you add stay on your device. You do not need an AliasTrail account to use the extension.

We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use AliasTrail, the extension may store locally:

  • Aliases and labels you enter
  • Sender or site notes you choose to save
  • Settings, such as warning preferences

This information remains on your device unless you export it or uninstall the extension.

Information we do not need

AliasTrail does not require access to your inbox or email password to provide its core features. We do not ask you to share your primary email account credentials with us.

Website and support

If you visit babyjappstudio.com, the site may collect standard analytics and diagnostic data, such as pages viewed, browser type, and approximate location derived from IP address. This is described in the company Privacy Policy.

If you email support, we receive the address and message you send so we can reply.

Purchases

Optional Pro upgrades, when available, are processed by the Chrome Web Store or another payment provider. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We may keep a record of whether a paid entitlement is active so Pro features can be unlocked.

Third parties

Google and the Chrome Web Store may collect install and usage information according to their own policies when you install AliasTrail from the store.

Children

AliasTrail is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of AliasTrail after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: babyjappstudio@outlook.com
