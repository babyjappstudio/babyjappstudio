Rank It

Delete your Rank It account

You can request deletion of your Rank It account and associated data at any time.

Email support to request deletion

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@babyjappstudio.com.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your Rank It account.
  3. Use the subject line: Rank It Account Deletion.

You do not need to reinstall Rank It to request deletion.

If you no longer have access to the email address associated with your account, you can still contact support. We may ask you to verify your identity.

What is deleted

After your request is verified, your Rank It account and associated account-linked data will be deleted.

What may be kept

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial-record purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Rank It account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions separately through the relevant app store.
