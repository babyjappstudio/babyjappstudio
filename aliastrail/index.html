AliasTrail

by BabyJ App Studio

Know where your email aliases travel.

Overview

Email aliases keep your real address off signup forms. AliasTrail helps you keep track of those aliases after you create them—so you can see how they are used, notice unexpected senders, and catch lookalike domains that try to impersonate a site you already trust.

How it works

1

Add your aliases

Save the aliases you already use for shopping, work, newsletters, and accounts.

2

3

Flag what looks off

Features

Alias usage at a glance

Keep a simple record of where each alias has shown up.

Unexpected sender review

See new or unfamiliar senders so reused or leaked aliases are easier to spot.

Lookalike domain alerts

Get a clear warning when a domain is close to one you already know.

Local-first by default

Your alias list stays on your device. No account is required to use AliasTrail.

Privacy

AliasTrail is built to keep alias details on your computer. The extension does not need access to your inbox to help you organize aliases, review senders you add, or check domains that look suspicious.

We do not sell your data. The BabyJ App Studio website may use standard analytics if you visit these pages. The extension is designed to work without creating an account.
